Hi! I want to solve challenging technical problems to help make people's lives better. I'm especially interested in sim2real learning for dexterous robot manipulation. I believe using simulation to scale robot training follows [the bitter lesson](http://www.incompleteideas.net/IncIdeas/BitterLesson.html) more faithfully than relying on real-world data alone.

I'm currently a PhD student advised by Prof. [Stefanie Tellex](https://www.linkedin.com/in/stefanie-tellex-38468818/) in Brown's [Humans to Robots Lab](https://h2r.cs.brown.edu/).

I did my undergrad at Northeastern, where I was advised by Prof. [Taskin Padir](https://www.linkedin.com/in/tpadir/) in the [RIVeR Lab](https://robot.neu.edu/).

My industry experience includes time at:

- Chewy Physical AI and Robotics
- [Robotics & AI Institute (formerly Boston Dynamics AI Institute)](https://rai-inst.com/)
- [Amazon Robotics](https://www.linkedin.com/company/amazon-fulfillment-technologies-robotics/p)
- Robotics & Autonomous Systems Lab at [General Electric Research](https://www.linkedin.com/company/geresearch/)
